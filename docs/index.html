Why convert HEIC to JPG offline?

Many online conversion services require photos to be uploaded to a remote server. OfflineHEIC lets you convert HEIC to JPG offline with local processing on your Windows PC, so no upload is needed.

What is HEIC?

HEIC is an image format commonly used for iPhone photos. Converting HEIC to JPG can make those photos easier to use with Windows apps and services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert HEIC to JPG without uploading my photos?

Yes. OfflineHEIC processes photos locally on your Windows PC, with no upload.

Does OfflineHEIC work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once installed, the conversion works offline without an internet connection.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files or folders?

Yes. You can select or drag and drop multiple HEIC files, or choose a folder to convert its HEIC files.
